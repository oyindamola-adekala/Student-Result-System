About Result Portal

The Academic Result Management System (ARMS) is a web-based application designed to help students and institutions manage academic records efficiently, securely, and accurately.

Our Features

📊 Result Management

Add, edit and organize academic results easily.

🧮 CGPA Calculator

Automatically calculate CGPA with accuracy.

📈 Performance Tracking

Monitor academic performance over time.

🔒 Secure Login

Your account and records are protected.

How It Works

  1. Create an account.
  2. Log in securely.
  3. Manage your academic records.
  4. Track your performance and CGPA.
